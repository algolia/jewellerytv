What is the Concierge? #

The Concierge is the shopping assistant built into the JTV website. A shopper describes what they are looking for in their own words. The Concierge asks a helpful question when that makes sense, searches the live JTV catalogue, and shows real pieces with their prices and stock.

It is an AI agent hosted by Algolia. That means it is not a menu and not a set of canned answers: it reads each shopper's request, decides what to do next, and writes its own replies in JTV's voice. Everything it is allowed to say and do is written in its instructions, which live in Algolia's Agent Studio dashboard.

What it does for a shopper #

A shopper can say things like "I'm looking for an anniversary gift for my wife" or "show me gold hoop earrings". The Concierge talks it through: it asks who the gift is for when that matters, explains jewellery terms in plain language, and shows a first set of real pieces rather than asking question after question. Shoppers can save pieces they like, compare them side by side, and ask for education, such as what a pavé setting is, drawn from JTV's own help and blog content.

Two promises sit underneath everything it says. First, every product, price and stock claim comes from the live catalogue; the Concierge does not invent pieces or make up availability. Second, the Concierge is honest about limits: if it cannot do something, such as place an order or check today's TV schedule, it says so and points the shopper to what it can do.

How a question becomes an answer #

Three things work together: the shopper, the JTV website, and the Concierge itself. Behind the Concierge sits the catalogue, the live product data every answer is grounded in. One exchange flows like this:

The same eight steps, in words:

  1. The shopper types what they want into the chat on the JTV website.
  2. The website passes the message to the Concierge, and the reply starts streaming back while it is still being written.
  3. When the Concierge needs products, it searches the live catalogue. It can narrow by price band, category, material and more.
  4. The search results themselves become the display: the pieces the search returned are the product tiles, and the catalogue's facet counts become the selectable narrowing options.
  5. Before a tile shows, the website re-checks its record: in stock, priced, photographed, and not a same-design twin of a tile already showing.
  6. The Concierge writes its reply from those results in JTV's voice. A safety check then reviews what it wrote; anything that breaks the rules is replaced before it stays on screen.
  7. The shopper sees the answer next to the approved product cards.
